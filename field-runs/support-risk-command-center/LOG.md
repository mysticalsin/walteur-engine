# Work Log

Append one short entry when a meaningful bulk of work ships or a loop completes a run.
Keep detail in linked artifacts, not here.

## Entry Grammar

```text
## YYYY-MM-DD - Short title - #tag
What: Outcome first, one or two lines.
Refs: [artifact](path) plus PR, commit, report, or run evidence.
```

## Rules

- Newest entries go at the bottom.
- One header per entry, starting with `## 20`.
- Link the proof: report, artifact, commit, PR, or test output.
- Do not paste secrets, private data, or long logs.
